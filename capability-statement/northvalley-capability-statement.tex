\NVISection{Past performance}
\SubH{Canon Insurance Advisers}
\NVIBody{\textbf{Client:} Canon Insurance Advisers, LLC (Georgia insurance agency).}\par
\NVIBody{\textbf{Role:} direct engagement, founder as sole developer.}\par
\NVIBody{\textbf{Period:} July 2026 to present (currently active).}\par
\NVIBody{\textbf{Situation:} a Georgia insurance agency quoted each home and auto prospect by hand, re-keying the same details for several hours per quote.}\par
\NVIBody{\textbf{Task:} cut quoting time without replacing the agency's existing tools.}\par
\NVIBody{\textbf{Action:} a one-page intake form feeds an automated quoting run that completes the rating steps unattended, with guard rails that never bind, purchase or order a report; an earlier phase added a retention board that flags policies at risk of lapsing before they end.}\par
\NVIBody{\textbf{Result:} several hours of manual quoting became a few seconds of form filling and a few minutes of automated quote generation.}\par
\NVIBody{\textbf{Reference:} available on request.}\par

\SubH{Smart Cash Sheet}
\NVIBody{\textbf{Client:} Smart Cash Sheet.}\par
\NVIBody{\textbf{Role:} ongoing engineering.}\par
\NVIBody{\textbf{Period:} September 2026 to present (currently active).}\par
\NVIBody{\textbf{Situation:} a personal cash-flow app on the web with iOS and Android apps, run by a solo founder, shipped about once a month, each release carrying weeks of accumulated change and risk.}\par
